About

Security work with a builder's bias.

I am focused on security research, automation, and defensive tooling. I care about systems that are practical to operate, documentable after the fact, and honest about tradeoffs.

The goal is straightforward: ship useful work, publish what matters, and keep improving the quality of both the engineering and the thinking behind it.

Operating principles

  • Document experiments before they become folklore.
  • Prefer small reliable tools over oversized frameworks.
  • Write clearly enough that future me can reuse the work.
  • Treat visual design with the same care as implementation.
