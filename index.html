About

I work on security problems where systems trust the wrong thing.

I focus on cyber-physical systems, anomaly detection, and cryptography when the real question is how a system behaves once it starts relying on the wrong assumption.

Most of the work here follows the same thread: understanding how trusted inputs, models, or cryptographic assumptions shape system behavior, and what happens when that trust fails quietly.

Focus Cyber-physical security, trusted data, and applied cryptography
Research PULSER, LiDAR spoofing, IEEE HOST 2026
