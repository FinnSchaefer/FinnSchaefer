ICS and grid-security direction

The next step in my work is taking the trust and perception questions behind PULSER into IT/OT and ICS environments, especially systems tied to electrical-grid telemetry and control.

What draws me to this direction is the continuity with the work I am already doing. PULSER is fundamentally about trusted input and what happens when that trust is misplaced. That same problem appears outside robotics any time operational systems depend on telemetry they assume is timely, accurate, and safe to act on.

Electrical-grid environments make that especially interesting. The gap between signal, interpretation, and control is small, and the operational consequences of bad data can be outsized even when nothing looks obviously broken at first glance.

I am interested in the systems side of that problem: how trust is established, how subtle manipulation moves through monitoring and decision logic, and how those failures should shape the way we think about cyber-physical defense in IT/OT environments.

This is not a separate interest from the rest of the site. It is the same thread carried into a harder setting. That is why I wanted it represented as its own page rather than a single card label on the homepage.
