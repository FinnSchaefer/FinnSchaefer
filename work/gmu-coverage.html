George Mason coverage of NTCC

The George Mason article gave public shape to the NTCC result and helped place that work in a broader context beyond the competition itself.

Recognition like this matters less to me as a headline and more because it helps explain the kind of security work I want to be associated with. The NTCC result was not about a single challenge or a narrow trick. It reflected a way of working across anomaly detection, applications, and operational systems under pressure.

The article also helps connect the result back to where my interests are moving. Transportation security is one of those domains where cyber, physical systems, data quality, and operational impact all collide quickly. That overlap is the same reason I am interested in cyber-physical security more broadly.

I wanted this page on the site because external coverage tells a slightly different story than a technical write-up. The write-up explains what I learned from the work. Coverage shows how that work lands when other people look at it from the outside.
